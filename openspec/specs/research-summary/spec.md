# research-summary Specification

## Purpose
Produces a readable generated briefing on a scored lead, assembled only from facts the platform already holds, with every claim traceable to one of them, an explicit statement of what is unknown, and no assertion about a company's finances, headcount or plans.
## Requirements
### Requirement: A summary is built only from stored facts

The system SHALL supply the model with the lead's stored company attributes, observations, technology detections, opportunities, contacts, score components and explanation, and nothing else. It SHALL NOT perform any retrieval, fetch, or search while generating a summary.

#### Scenario: Only stored facts are supplied

- **WHEN** a summary is generated
- **THEN** the model input contains only facts already persisted for that lead

#### Scenario: No retrieval occurs

- **WHEN** a summary is generated
- **THEN** no network request other than the model call is made

#### Scenario: Another company's facts are never included

- **WHEN** a summary is generated for one lead
- **THEN** the input contains no data belonging to another company

### Requirement: Every claim cites a supplied fact

The system SHALL require each claim in the summary to cite the identifier of a fact that was supplied. After parsing, the system SHALL verify each citation against the actual input set and SHALL reject any claim citing nothing or citing an identifier that was not supplied.

#### Scenario: A cited claim is retained

- **WHEN** a claim cites an identifier present in the input
- **THEN** it is retained in the stored summary

#### Scenario: An uncited claim is rejected

- **WHEN** a claim cites no fact
- **THEN** it is removed from the summary and the rejection is recorded

#### Scenario: A fabricated citation is rejected

- **WHEN** a claim cites an identifier that was not supplied
- **THEN** it is removed from the summary and the rejection is recorded

#### Scenario: A summary left with no claims is not stored as a summary

- **WHEN** every claim in a response is rejected
- **THEN** no summary is stored and the run records a failed outcome with the rejection counts

### Requirement: Prohibited claims are rejected however they are phrased

The system SHALL reject any summary content asserting a company's revenue, profit, funding, valuation, headcount, hiring activity, customer count, growth rate, budget or strategic plans. The check SHALL be applied by the system to the parsed output and SHALL NOT rely on the model having followed an instruction.

#### Scenario: A revenue claim is rejected

- **WHEN** the model returns a claim asserting the company's revenue or turnover
- **THEN** that claim is removed and the rejection is recorded

#### Scenario: A headcount claim is rejected

- **WHEN** the model returns a claim asserting how many people the company employs
- **THEN** that claim is removed and the rejection is recorded

#### Scenario: A plans claim is rejected

- **WHEN** the model returns a claim asserting what the company intends to do
- **THEN** that claim is removed and the rejection is recorded

#### Scenario: A stored company size is not a prohibited claim

- **WHEN** the platform holds a company size from a cited source and the summary repeats it with that citation
- **THEN** the claim is retained

### Requirement: What is unknown is stated explicitly

The system SHALL require the summary to include the facts the platform does not hold for this lead, and SHALL populate that list from the platform's own record of absent values rather than from the model's judgement.

#### Scenario: Absent facts are listed

- **WHEN** a lead has no contact with a known email and no company size
- **THEN** the summary's unknown list names both

#### Scenario: The unknown list is derived, not generated

- **WHEN** the summary is stored
- **THEN** its unknown list matches the platform's record of absent values for that lead

#### Scenario: A complete lead has an empty unknown list

- **WHEN** every fact the summary covers is held
- **THEN** the unknown list is empty rather than omitted

### Requirement: A summary is labelled as generated and traceable

The system SHALL store every summary marked as generated by a model, with the prompt version and the invocation that produced it, and SHALL return that marking with the summary wherever it is read.

#### Scenario: A summary is marked as generated

- **WHEN** a summary is read
- **THEN** it is marked as generated by a model

#### Scenario: A summary references its invocation and prompt version

- **WHEN** a stored summary is inspected
- **THEN** the invocation and prompt version that produced it are retrievable

### Requirement: Regeneration retains the previous summary

The system SHALL allow a summary to be regenerated, SHALL retain the previous ones, and SHALL identify the most recent as current.

#### Scenario: Regeneration keeps history

- **WHEN** a lead's summary is regenerated
- **THEN** both summaries exist and the newer is current

#### Scenario: A failed regeneration leaves the previous summary current

- **WHEN** regeneration fails
- **THEN** the previously current summary remains current
- **AND** the failure is recorded

### Requirement: A summary is not produced for an unscored lead

The system SHALL refuse to generate a summary for a company with no lead or with a lead that has never been scored, naming the reason, and SHALL make no model call.

#### Scenario: An unscored company is refused

- **WHEN** a summary is requested for a company with no scored lead
- **THEN** the request is refused naming the reason
- **AND** no model call is made
